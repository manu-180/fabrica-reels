# Tone for @apex.webssites

For small business owners in the US who have no website yet, or an outdated one. APEX builds them a
free custom demo, and they only pay the first installment once they have seen it and love it.

## How it sounds

- Natural American English, the way a friendly local web pro talks: short, concrete, a little
  funny. No agency jargon, no leftover Spanish.
- Talk about what happens to the business (customers who leave, the same questions every day, the
  nephew who never finishes the site), not about technology.
- The offer only as the `oferta` facts say it: free demo, first installment once you love it,
  deadlines in writing. Never prices in the reel.
- The "Comment WEB" close is added by the pipeline: it never goes in the voiceover or the captions.
- No dashes, no emojis, no numbered blocks. Businesses and names are always made up, never a lead
  or a client.

## Good

- "If texting you means copying your number first, a lot of people just won't."
- "Your website is the storefront people check from their phone."

## Not like this

- "We build scalable web solutions with on-page SEO." Jargon a shop owner does not buy.
- "Your site for 600 dollars." Prices stay out of the reel: the chat handles them.
